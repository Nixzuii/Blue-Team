# Introduction to SOC.

**SOC** or **Security Operation Center** is the centralized command hub where an organization's cybersecurity defense is planned, executed, and refined. It's not just a room full of monitors — it's a combination of people, processes, and technology working 24/7 to protect digital assets. The SOC's mission can be summed up as: monitor, detect, analyze, and respond to threats in real time, while continuously improving the organization's security posture. Unlike ad-hoc or "reactive only" security, a mature SOC is proactive — it hunts for threats, identifies weaknesses before attackers exploit them, and refines defenses based on every incident. The core functions that define what a SOC does on a daily basis are:

### 1. Identify

- Recognize an alert as potentially malicious.
- Open an incident ticket and begin tracking.
- Use tools like SIEM, XDR, and threat intelligence feeds to spot anomalies.
